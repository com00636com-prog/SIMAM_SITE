صِمام

Intrusion detection that runs where the network isn't.

A passive module beside a PLC or RTU at an unmanned site. It listens to one device's traffic and decides on a 256 KB microcontroller, with no cloud and no uplink in the detection path. Below, the real exported models run in your browser on real benchmark records, the controller is opened up, and every result of the paper can be explored, including the ones that went against us.

§ 01The gap

Thousands of control devices run real processes, and nobody is watching them.

Programmable logic controllers, remote terminal units and field sensors at pipeline stations, tank farms and fuel-station sites cannot host a security agent. A network appliance is too much for a small site; a cloud service needs an uplink the site often does not have. Two commercial answers exist, and both leave the same kind of site uncovered.

آلاف أجهزة التحكّم تدير عمليات حقيقية، ولا أحد يراقبها. الحلول التجارية تغطي حالتين فقط، والموقع الصغير غير المأهول خارجهما.

§ 02How it works

Two small neural networks, a gate between them, and a controller that decides how hard to look.

Each packet becomes 44 numeric fields. An autoencoder trained only on benign traffic scores how unfamiliar the record is; a classifier says attack or normal; a nine-rule fuzzy-logic controller sets the duty cycle and the gate threshold from threat level, energy budget and network load. The architecture is the published SMEESI design, reviewed and adapted.

شبكتان عصبيتان صغيرتان، وبوّابة بينهما، ومتحكّم منطقي ضبابي يقرّر كم نُشغّل من الحماية.
1 · Listen

Frames arrive on a mirror port or a fail-open passive tap. The firmware pulls 44 numeric fields per packet: ports, flags, lengths, Modbus, MQTT and DNS header fields. No payload is stored.

2 · Autoencoder

44–128–32–64–128–44, 25,996 parameters, 25,600 multiply-accumulates. Trained on benign traffic only; reconstruction error is the anomaly score.

3 · Gate

Error above the threshold wakes the classifier; otherwise the module logs one line and sleeps. Paper v4.0 also evaluates the inverted, classifier-first order.

4 · Classifier

44–64–32–1, 4,993 parameters, 4,896 multiply-accumulates. 9,864 bytes as a full-integer TensorFlow Lite model, 98.0% accuracy through the interpreter.

5 · Alert

Local LED and hash-chained log always; a one-line message to SCADA when an uplink exists. The module never transmits into the control path.

Controller

Nine fuzzy rules read threat level, energy budget and network load; the output sets the duty cycle and the gate strictness k = clamp(4 − 2.5P, 1.5, 4).

Concept design and animated installation walkthrough. The prototype for the competitions is the bare Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense on a bench, receiving records over USB; the Ethernet interface, housing and 24 V regulator are design, not yet built. Mirror-port mode has no transmit path; inline mode uses a fail-open relay, so if the module fails, production continues.

Lab · BThe fuzzy controller, opened up

Three readings in. One decision out: how hard to look.

Threat level, energy budget and network load enter as numbers in [0, 1]; each is split into low, medium and high by the membership functions of Appendix A of the paper; nine rules fire with the strength of their weakest condition; the centre of gravity of the fired outputs is the processing intensity P. Power = 6 + 36·P mW is the source paper's model, bounded by the nRF52840 datasheet (52 µA/MHz at 64 MHz ≈ 11 mW CPU-active). It has not been measured on our board, and it excludes the Ethernet interface, which alone draws 43–420 mW (§04). What this simulator is and is not. Detections are per-packet Bernoulli draws at the measured recall; false alarms are Poisson draws at the measured false-positive rate; the daytime traffic shape (night at 40% of the peak) is an assumption; power is the 6 + 36·P model plus the W5500 datasheet current, not a measurement. Two honest lessons it shows: a flood is caught within the first minute at any configuration, while a low-and-slow attack at a packet a minute can wait minutes for its first alert and, at the identifier-blind configuration, has most of its packets never alerted; and at ten packets per second even a 0.013% false-positive rate is about a hundred false alarms a day, which is why alert aggregation and a per-site false-alarm budget are design requirements.

§ 03Results

98.6% on the benchmark. 90–91% once port numbers are taken away. Both are in the paper.

Edge-IIoTset, 2,219,201 packet records, binary task. Pick a split and a feature set. The chart is to scale; the majority-class baseline is 72.8%. The dataset's authors report 99.98% on the same task; a depth-3 decision tree on port numbers alone scores 97.5%. The port-blind figure is the one to expect at a facility with unfamiliar port assignments, and it is the figure the paper regards as its honest headline.

§ 04Explorers

Threshold, decision rule, false alarms, power, cost. Move the controls; the numbers recombine measured values.

Each panel answers one question from the paper. Where a figure is an estimate rather than a measurement it says so on the panel.

العتبة، قاعدة القرار، الإنذارات الكاذبة، الطاقة، التكلفة. كل لوحة تجيب عن سؤال واحد من الورقة.
Threshold explorer the gate's recall depends on the rule you choose

The source paper's mean + 3 SD rule sits at the 99.98th percentile of benign reconstruction error: far too strict for a heavy-tailed distribution. A 99th-percentile threshold lifts gate recall from 57.5% to 78.7% at a false-positive rate of 0.95%, and the gated pipeline (classifier confirming) to 78.7% at 0.015%. Random split, full features.

Decision rule gated (v3.1) versus classifier-first (v4.0)

False alarms at scale

None of these rates is acceptable as a raw alert stream. Alert aggregation on the module, a 24-hour per-site benign measurement before alerts are forwarded, and a false-alarm budget agreed with the operator are design requirements, not options.

Power: the Ethernet interface sets the floor

Until whole-module current is measured, SIMAM makes no power claim of its own. The 42 mW in the source paper is that paper's figure for its own setup, and the Ethernet interface alone exceeds it.

Cost, expressed in downtime hours worked example, not a business case

Per device: board €43.40 (Arduino store) ≈ SAR 190; housing and interface SAR 60–90, passive tap SAR 100–300 and one hour of labour SAR 150 are estimates. No discounting, no maintenance, no alert-handling cost, no probability of incident. Electricity: a duty-cycled microcontroller against an always-on 105 mW monitor saves a few thousand riyals a year across the whole fleet at the industrial tariff. The money is in avoided or shortened stoppages, and that is an upper bound on value, not an expectation.

§ 05Threat model

Five assumptions, and what happens when each one fails.

Attacker on the facility segment: a compromised engineering workstation, a rogue device, or a pivot from the corporate network. Can send Modbus/TCP, MQTT, DNS, HTTP and ICMP, spoof, replay, scan and flood. Cannot touch the module. May know the detector exists.

خمس افتراضات، وما يحدث عند فشل كل منها. المهاجم على شبكة الموقع ولا يستطيع لمس الوحدة.
AssumptionIf it failsMitigation in the design
Mirror port or tap is receive-onlyModule compromise could reach productionNo transmit path in mirror mode; fail-open relay inline; the module never transmits on MONITOR IN
Calibration traffic is cleanAn attacker present during calibration becomes "normal"Calibrate in a period the historian confirms quiet; percentile cap; operator approval to recalibrate; the classifier does not depend on site calibration
Attacker does not know the thresholdTraffic kept inside the benign envelope passes the gateA third of dataset attacks already reconstruct like benign records; the gate is a first filter; the supervised stage and protocol rules carry known attacks
Alerts reach someoneWith no uplink and no visit, an alert is a log lineLocal LED and log always; uplink message when available; false-alarm budget before routing to a SOC
Site traffic resembles training trafficPorts and protocols differ by sitePort-blind results are the expected starting point; per-site recalibration managed across the fleet

Out of scope: serial field buses, encrypted payloads, attacks with no network footprint, attacks that stay on the IT side. The module's own hardening (signed firmware, no radio, hash-chained log, authenticated alerts, fail-open) is specified, not yet implemented.

§ 06Roadmap

Ordered by what each step lets the paper claim.

خارطة الطريق مرتّبة بما تتيحه كل خطوة من ادّعاء في الورقة.
1 · Public-file evaluation

Leave-one-attack-family-out, host-disjoint and time-ordered splits; per-family recall; percentile thresholds and the classifier-first rule on those splits; five seeds per cell. Needs a laptop only.

2 · Behaviour-level features

Per (master, device) window features the module can compute, and a learned Modbus allowlist as the non-learning baseline.

3 · Bench rig and measurement

Small PLC, mirror-port switch, microcontroller with Ethernet; whole-module current in idle, gate-only and full-inference states; sustained packet rate; live attacks from a laptop. The hardware-in-the-loop replay in the repository (the same 2,000 records as Lab A) is the first step.

4 · ICS data and pilot

A Modbus or gas-pipeline dataset with process context; then one non-safety-critical segment with an operator for recalibration and a real false-alarm count.

§ 07What we do not claim

The list that makes the rest of the page believable.

ما لا ندّعيه. هذه القائمة هي ما يجعل بقية الصفحة جديرة بالتصديق.
  • No power, latency or memory has been measured by the team; every such figure is the source paper's or a datasheet bound.
  • Nothing has run on the microcontroller yet; on-device feature extraction and the sustainable packet rate are unmeasured. Lab A runs the exported models in your browser, which shows the models are real and complete, not that the board meets any timing.
  • The benchmark is IoT/IIoT testbed traffic that is separable largely by port numbers, not a facility network; records are single packets.
  • Per-family recall and held-out-family generalisation have not been measured, although the public file permits them.
  • The classifier-first rule's anomaly path found no attack the classifier missed on this dataset.
  • Lab C and the cost panel rest on stated assumptions and are demonstration material, not results.
